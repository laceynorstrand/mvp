Warm welcomes, clear agreements

Research says trust decides whether anyone says yes. So the match comes with checks, a written agreement, and a clean way out.

  1. Verify identityPhoto ID and a background check for hosts and lodgers.
  2. Check referencesTwo references, one personal and one from a landlord, employer or professor.
  3. Match on fitA short compatibility questionnaire: sleep, noise, guests, meals, help offered.
  4. Sign the agreementRent, help hours, quiet hours and privacy written down before move-in.
  5. Check in and exitScheduled check-ins, a mediator, and a 30-day notice option for either side.
