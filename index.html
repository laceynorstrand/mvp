How it works

Two short paths that meet in the middle: a questionnaire on each side, a person on our team who reads both, and a match we suggest only when the checks are done.

For young people

Find a home, not just a room

  1. Browse and say helloSee rent, expected help and house rules on every listing before you reach out.
  2. Fill in the questionnaireFive minutes on your routines, allergies and the help you can offer.
  3. Get checkedID, a background check and two references. Hosts go through the same.
  4. Meet, then decideA video call, then a visit. Nothing is binding until you both sign.
  5. Move in with supportCheck-ins at week 2, month 1 and every term, and a mediator if you need one.
For hosts

Share a room, gain a housemate

  1. List your spaceSet the rent, the help you would like and your house rules. You see the listing as you type.
  2. Fill in the questionnaireTell us about your household rhythm and anything present in the home, like pets.
  3. Confirm the basicsWorking smoke detectors, a lockable door, permission to rent and a heads-up to your insurer.
  4. Meet one or two matchesWe suggest people whose routines fit yours. You choose whether to go further.
  5. Sign and settle inA plain-language agreement covers rent, help hours, quiet hours and how either of you can leave.

Questions people ask

The worries that came up most when we talked to students and homeowners.

Is the lodger expected to be a caregiver?

No. Help is ordinary household help like cooking together, garden work or shoveling snow, capped in the agreement. Personal or medical care is never part of a TwoGen stay.

What if we do not get along?

Either side can raise it at a scheduled check-in, ask for a mediator, or give 30 days' written notice. If safety is at risk, the stay can end sooner.

How do you decide who to match?

Both sides fill in the same questionnaire. We compare daily rhythms, the help offered and wanted, and any allergy conflicts, then a person on our team reviews the best fits before anyone is introduced.

Who can be a lodger?

Anyone 18 or older with a verifiable reason to be in Bozeman: MSU students, international newcomers, new hires and interns. Everyone passes ID, background and reference checks.

Do I need my landlord's or insurer's permission to host?

If you rent, you need written permission from your landlord. Homeowners should tell their insurer they are renting a room. Both are on the host checklist.

Who can see my personal information?

Listings show only a general area, a nickname you choose and the room details. Your name, email and address are seen by the TwoGen team only. A match learns your first name after you both agree to meet.

What does TwoGen cost?

In this prototype, nothing. A launched service would likely charge a small matching fee once a stay is signed, so nobody pays to browse or apply.

Have a room, and room in your life?

Tell us about the space. We will read every sign-up ourselves and suggest one or two possible matches.
